Getting around and looking after your equipment are essential for delivery work. The Houses help in several ways.

## What you can find in a House

- **Bike self-repair garage**, to repair your bike yourself, with rules on battery repair and bike storage to prevent fire risks.
- **Bike mechanics workshops**.
- **Road safety prevention**: workshops to learn about road risks and how to avoid them, with associations or city services.
- **Bike fitting workshop**: adjust the height of the handlebars and saddle, equipment advice.
- **Access to equipment**: group purchases or free equipment (helmet, reflective clothing, more ergonomic bags).

In the SANTE-COURSE survey, more than half of the delivery workers surveyed had already had an accident at work.
