In independent delivery work, many people leave because of hard working conditions and low income. The Houses help delivery workers who want to leave in a lasting way.

## What you can find in a House

- **Help writing a CV and cover letters**, with templates and tutorials on free-access computers.
- **Referral** to France Travail (the public employment service), training organisations, Missions Locales (youth employment services), Espaces emploi (job centres) or work-integration companies.
- **Group recruitment sessions** with companies looking for employed delivery workers.
- **French and computer classes**, so you can learn without paying too much.

## Partner organisations

- **Espace Proximité Emploi**: job information, advice on career change and training, individual or group support.
- **BimBamJob**: social and professional support, advice and training.
- **Missions Locales**: support for young people aged 16 to 25, guidance, training, help to find a job.
- **Local bike cooperatives**: for example Coursiers Bordelais, Sicklo, Narvélos.
