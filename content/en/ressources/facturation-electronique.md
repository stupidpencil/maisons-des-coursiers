## A problem?

### My business does not appear

Check your SIRET number, digit by digit. You can find it on annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr by typing your name.

### My ID is refused

Take the photo again in daylight. No glare, no blur. The whole document must be visible.

### I left before the end

No problem. Go back to **Importations** (Imports), then **Factures électroniques** (E-invoices). Continue where you stopped.

### I am stuck

Ask the team that supports you for help.

## Why it is useful

Since 1 September 2026, all businesses, including micro-businesses, must be able to receive e-invoices. Shine Facturation does it for you for free.
