Delivery workers face many difficulties at work: frequent deactivations and abuses on platforms, or problems with another employer when delivery is not their only job. Being in contact with unions helps you get support.

## What you can find in a House

- **Help if your account is deactivated**: some unions act as go-betweens between the platform and the deactivated delivery worker.
- **Union advice sessions** to settle disputes at work. In a regularisation through work (getting papers based on employment), their support can also help to get the work authorisation from the employer.

## Unions that work with delivery workers

CLAP, SUD Commerces et Services, CGT, FNAE, CNT Solidarité ouvrière, Union Indépendants, Solidaires and Force ouvrière. Their positions differ. Some see platform work as disguised employment. Others defend delivery workers' self-employed status.

In Paris, CNT-SO and Sud Commerces hold advice sessions at the Maison des Coursiers.
