Delivery work exposes people to health risks: road accidents, pain from the saddle and heavy bags, stress, tiredness. In the SANTE-COURSE survey, one in three delivery workers gave up medical care in the last twelve months. Problems with papers and language are among the reasons.

## What you can find in a House

- **Access to care**: check that you are covered (Aide médicale d'État, AME, or the general health insurance scheme), get supplementary health cover if possible, understand how to book an appointment.
- **Health advice sessions**: consultations in a place you know, with a prescription or a care pathway to follow.
- **Mental health sessions**, individual or in a group.
- **Free testing** for HIV and hepatitis, sexual health awareness, free condoms.

## Partner organisations

- **Médecins du Monde**: volunteer doctors, health advice sessions, support to access rights.
- **Comede**: access to care and health coverage, right to stay for medical reasons, health check-ups.
- **AIDES**: condom distribution, sexual health sessions.
- **Others**: health centres, CeGIDD (free sexual health clinics), Lunettes pour tous (glasses for all), mobile care services, the city's PASS (free hospital care access for people without cover), mobile psychiatric teams for people in precarious situations.
