## A project being prepared

The association of the Maison des Livreurs in Nantes was created in July 2025. It is preparing to open a place to meet, rest and take a break for delivery workers in the Nantes area. Opening is planned for September 2026, with activities growing step by step.

## In the meantime

- **Outreach rounds** to meet delivery workers where they wait for orders.
- **Repair workshops** for bikes and scooters.
- **Support with rights**, with partner associations.
- A **test space** hosts advice sessions.

The project is run with Naofood, le Coup de Main Numérique, Berekty (social interpreting) and Commun'Île.
