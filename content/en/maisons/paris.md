## A place for rest and support

The Maison des Coursiers started from an initiative of elected officials of the City of Paris and the association CoopCycle. Talks began in 2020 and the place opened in September 2021. It is located in the middle of the places where Paris delivery workers wait for orders (Châtelet, Place de Clichy, République, Bastille).

## Services

- **Rest area**: hot drinks, toilets, battery charging, rest room.
- **Administrative procedures**: tax returns, quarterly URSSAF declaration (self-employed social contributions), procedures with the CAF (family allowance office).
- **Right to stay**: procedures with the préfecture (local state office), first applications for a residence permit.
- **Legal support** with partner lawyers, and help for accident victims.
- **Health**: consultations with a nurse, HIV and hepatitis testing, prevention, mental health.
- **Job support**: employment workshop, French and computer classes (2 hours per week each).
- **Labour law**: contact with unions and union advice sessions.

## Who runs the House

The House is co-managed by CoopCycle Association, the Centre d'action sociale protestant (CASP) and CIEL, the delivery workers' collective. Volunteers also run administrative advice sessions and classes.
