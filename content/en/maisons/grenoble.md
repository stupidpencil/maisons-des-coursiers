## A space won through struggle

This space opened in July 2024, after three years of struggle by Grenoble delivery workers. After the demonstrations of December 2023 for better working conditions, and with the support of the City of Grenoble, delivery workers won a space open day and night.

## Services

- **Rest area**.
- **French classes**.
