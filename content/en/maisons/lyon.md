## A project being prepared

CoopCycle and La Ville à Vélo are preparing a Maison des coursiers in Lyon and Villeurbanne. It will welcome platform bike delivery workers.

- **First, a place to rest**: get out of the weather, have a coffee, charge your phone, use the toilet.
- **Then, a place for support**: help with paperwork, with physical and mental health, and with your rights.

## A first trial year

The project starts with a one-year trial, planned from April 2027. One person will hold a 7-hour weekly drop-in session in a partner venue, to:

- **Welcome**: coffee, water, toilets, phone charging.
- **Help with paperwork**: taxes, URSSAF, CAF, social housing.
- **Refer**: to unions, lawyers and legal advisers, health services.
- **Open the way to jobs**: CVs, recruitment, discovering bike jobs, bike logistics or bike repair.

The partner venue has not been chosen yet. Opening hours and the address will be published here.

## Who is behind the project

- **CoopCycle**: a network of bike delivery cooperatives. It created the Maison des coursiers in Paris.
- **La Ville à Vélo**: an association that makes cycling accessible to everyone in the Lyon metropolitan area.

The project builds on the experience of the Maisons already open, which share their tools and advice.
