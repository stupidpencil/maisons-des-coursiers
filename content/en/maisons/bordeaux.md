## A place born from delivery workers' mobilisation

The Maison des Livreurs started from an initiative of the delivery workers' collective AMAL (Association de mobilisation et d'accompagnement des livreurs), and of the associations EtuRécup, Médecins du Monde and CoopCycle. Talks began in 2022 and the place opened in early 2023. The premises are provided by the City.

## Services

- **Rest area** and free-access computer to do your paperwork.
- **Help with administrative procedures** and legal advice sessions.
- **Bike self-repair garage**.
- **Health**: medical consultations and health mediation.
- **Road safety prevention**.
- **Connection with unions**, help with finding work and with training.
- **Computer classes**.

The place is very busy. Its premises are overcrowded.
