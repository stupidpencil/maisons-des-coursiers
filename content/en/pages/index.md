::faq
---
title: FAQ
description: "The most common questions, to better understand the Maison des Coursiers."
items:
  - question: Who is the Maison des Coursiers for?
    answer: "The Maison des Coursiers is for all platform delivery workers, whatever their status or administrative situation. It especially welcomes precarious workers, often self-employed, sometimes undocumented, who have few resources and limited access to rights."
  - question: What does the Maison des Coursiers offer in practice?
    answer: "The Maison des Coursiers is a free place that combines several functions:"
    points:
      - A rest area (coffee, water, toilets)
      - Help with paperwork (CAF, taxes, URSSAF, prefecture)
      - Access to care (consultations, prevention, referral)
      - Support for job integration (employment, training)
      - A link to unions and workers' collectives
  - question: Why create a place dedicated to delivery workers?
    answer: "Delivery workers work in public space with no dedicated infrastructure. They face:"
    points:
      - risky working conditions,
      - significant isolation,
      - and limited access to public services.
    after: "The Maison des Coursiers addresses this gap by offering a stable anchor point, accessible and suited to their needs."
  - question: What is the real impact of the project?
    answer: "Since it opened:"
    points:
      - More than 1,700 delivery workers have been supported
      - About 30 procedures are completed every day
      - 78 work contracts have been signed with support
      - Access to care has been set up, with free consultations
    after: "Beyond the numbers, the Maison des Coursiers helps rebuild social bonds and secure life paths."
  - question: Is the place open to all delivery workers, even undocumented ones?
    answer: "Yes. The Maison des Coursiers welcomes people regardless of their administrative situation. The goal is to guarantee effective access to basic rights: health, information, support, dignity."
  - question: Is it a social or a political project?
    answer: "Both. It is a social project, because it meets immediate needs: resting, getting care, doing paperwork. It is also a political lever, because it documents working conditions and campaigns to improve delivery workers' rights."
  - question: How is the project funded?
    answer: "It runs on a mix of funding:"
    points:
      - support from local authorities (notably the City of Paris)
      - research funding and calls for projects
      - partnerships with associations
      - services and donations
    after: "This model remains fragile, with a strong dependence on public funding."
  - question: Can a Maison des Coursiers be replicated in another city?
    answer: "Yes. A replication kit has been designed to support local authorities and associations wishing to create a similar place. The team shares its expertise to make setting up easier and to adapt the project to local realities. Feel free to contact the Association for more information."
