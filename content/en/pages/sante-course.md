::numbered-list
---
headline: Proposals
title: What we are asking for
description: "A European directive on platform work must now become French law. With Médecins du Monde, the Maisons in Paris and Bordeaux are asking for:"
items:
  - title: Access to rights and care, whatever the status
    description: "Inform delivery workers about their health rights, make care easier to reach, and have the national health insurance cover work accidents."
  - title: Better working conditions
    description: "Know how the app works, have a person to talk to, be paid for the real working time and the real distance, with a minimum income. Fewer unjustified police checks."
  - title: A stable status that allows people to work
    description: "Be able to choose between being an employee or self-employed. Count delivery work, even under someone else's account, for regularisation. Regularise delivery workers without papers."
---
