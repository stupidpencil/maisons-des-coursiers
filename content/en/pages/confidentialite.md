# Privacy policy

This website asks you for nothing and does not track you. Here is what happens when you visit it or write to us.

## In short

- **No account, no form.** You have nothing to fill in to use the website.
- **No tracking cookies, no audience measurement.** We do not know who visits the website.
- **No questions about your papers.** The Maisons welcome everyone, whatever your immigration status.

## Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is CoopCycle Association, the publisher of the website, presented in the [legal notice](/en/mentions-legales). For any question about your data: [mdc@coopcycle.org](mailto:mdc@coopcycle.org).

## What we process

### When you visit the website

We collect no data about you. Like any host, **Vercel** receives your IP address and technical information (browser, page requested) to display the website and protect it from attacks. We do not use this information.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in running the website and keeping it secure.
- **Provider:** Vercel Inc.

### When you look at a map

The maps use **OpenStreetMap** backgrounds. To display them, your browser contacts the OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address. We send them nothing else.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in showing where the Maisons are.
- **Provider:** OpenStreetMap Foundation.

### When you write to us

The website has no form. The "Contact the team" button shows our address: you write to us from your own email app, if you want to. We then keep your email address and your message to reply to you.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in answering requests.
- **Required or not:** you do not have to write to us. Without a message from you, we have no contact details about you.

For your safety, do not mention your immigration status in this first message. The Maisons will talk with you about it in person if you wish.

### Your display preference

Your choice of theme (light or dark) is saved in your browser. It is not sent to our servers.

## Cookies

This website sets **no tracking or advertising cookies**. That is why it shows no consent banner.

## How long do we keep your data?

- **Your messages:** 3 years after the last exchange, then they are deleted.
- **The host's technical information:** as long as needed to run and secure the website, under Vercel's rules.

## Who receives your data?

Your messages are read only by the Maisons des coursiers network team. We do not sell them or pass them on to anyone else.

Our technical providers are **Vercel Inc.** (hosting), in the United States, and the **OpenStreetMap Foundation** (map backgrounds), in the United Kingdom. Transfers to the United States are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. The United Kingdom is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data. You can also object to its use, or ask for its restriction or portability. To do so, write to [mdc@coopcycle.org](mailto:mdc@coopcycle.org).

If you think your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: [cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr).
